# Support

EMA Companion is free for anyone to use. The **Support** tab in the bottom navigation bar is always reachable — even before you've entered your EMA credentials — and offers two ways to support the developer:

- **Buy Me a Coffee** — opens the developer's Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser, where you can leave a small voluntary donation if you find the app helpful.
- **Visit Website** — opens the developer's personal website in your browser.

Both links open outside the app in your device's default browser. Nothing is required — the app is fully functional without ever visiting this screen.
